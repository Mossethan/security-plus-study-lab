A SMALL NOTE ON SOUND

Made to sit in the background.

Still creates original, evolving ambient audio in your browser. Mix soft musical layers with synthesized noise textures, or bring your own audio file.

The optional rhythmic pulse is amplitude modulation: a gentle repeating change in volume. The Hz setting describes that repetition rate, not the pitch of the music.

This is a personal sound experiment. Choose what feels comfortable; pulse can be turned down to zero.

Choose Pomodoro for repeating focus/break cycles, or Single session to stop after one focus period. In Pomodoro mode, focus audio fades out for breaks and returns for the next focus round. Keep this page open while the timer runs. Your settings and completed focus rounds stay in this browser. They do not sync between devices. Your audio files are never sent to a server. Choose an MP3 folder and filter by genre and neural-effect labels in filenames or folders. Unlabeled tracks remain available under All or Unlabeled. Duplicate cleanup is a separate local utility; the website does not scan for or remove duplicates. Reselect your files after reloading; the site cannot automatically browse your computer.
