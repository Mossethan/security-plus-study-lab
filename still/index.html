A SMALL NOTE ON SOUND

Made to sit in the background.

Still creates original, evolving ambient audio in your browser. Mix soft musical layers with synthesized noise textures, or bring your own audio file.

The optional rhythmic pulse is amplitude modulation: a gentle repeating change in volume. The Hz setting describes that repetition rate, not the pitch of the music.

This is a personal sound experiment inspired by focus-music tools. It does not reproduce Brain.fm’s music, proprietary methods, or demonstrated effects. Choose what feels comfortable; pulse can be turned down to zero.

Your settings and completed sessions stay in this browser. They do not sync between devices. Uploaded tracks are never sent to a server and need to be selected again after reloading.
